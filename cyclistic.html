Cyclistic Bike Share Analysis

Tools: Excel | SQL | R | Tableau

Project Overview

Result: Identified key behavioral differences between casual and member riders, enabling targeted marketing strategies to increase membership conversion.

This project analyzes Cyclistic bike-share data to understand differences between casual riders and annual members and identify strategies to increase membership conversions.

Business Problem

Cyclistic wants to increase annual memberships. Casual riders use the service but are not converting into members. This analysis identifies behavioral differences to support marketing strategies.

Key Insights

  • Members generated over 3.5M rides compared to 2.0M casual rides, showing higher engagement
  • Casual riders spend nearly twice as long per ride (22.6 vs 12.3 minutes)
  • Members ride primarily on weekdays, indicating commuting behavior
  • Casual riders prefer weekends and summer months, indicating leisure usage

Dashboard

Tools Used

Excel, PostgreSQL (SQL), R (tidyverse), Tableau

Recommendations

  • Target weekend casual riders with promotions
  • Introduce ride-based incentives
  • Launch seasonal campaigns
