Customer Churn Analysis

Tools: Excel | SQL | R | Tableau

Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a telecom company to identify key drivers of churn and recommend strategies to improve retention.

Result: Identified key drivers of churn and provided targeted strategies to reduce customer attrition.

Key Insights

  • Overall churn rate is 26.5%, indicating a significant retention issue
  • Month-to-month contracts show highest churn (42.7%)
  • New customers (0–12 months) have highest churn (47.4%)
  • Fiber optic users show high churn (41.9%)
  • Electronic check users have highest churn (45.3%)
  • Highest risk segment: Month-to-month + Electronic check (53.7%)

Dashboard

Recommendations

  • Promote long-term contracts with incentives
  • Improve fiber optic service quality and pricing
  • Focus on early customer retention strategies
  • Encourage automatic payment methods
