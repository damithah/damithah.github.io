Customer Churn Analysis

Business Problem: The company is experiencing a high churn rate, leading to revenue loss. Understanding which customers are most likely to leave and why is critical to developing effective retention strategies.

Key Results:

  • Approximately one in four customers leave the service, indicating a significant retention issue.
  • Customers without long-term contracts are significantly more likely to churn.
  • Customers using electronic check are significantly more likely to churn.
